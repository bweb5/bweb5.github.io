Vision-Based UAV Tracking

This project developed a vision-based UAV tracking simulation in MuJoCo using a downward-facing camera, color-based target detection, and image-based visual servoing. Instead of controlling the drone from known world coordinates, we used image error from the camera frame to generate planar velocity commands and keep the UAV over a moving platform.

The final system focused on a proportional IBVS baseline with an onboard camera view, an external debug view, scenario testing under fast motion and clutter, and a hardware-oriented command structure that could later connect to a real drone interface.

What We Did

We built a simulated UAV and moving platform, detected a red target marker from camera images, computed image error e_x and e_v, and mapped that error into planar velocity commands with a simple proportional IBVS controller.

We also tested the controller under slow, fast, dropout, breakaway, and cluttered scenarios to understand how well the baseline holds up.
